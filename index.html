        Make this your home page

        So your classes are the first thing you see every time you open Chrome.

        1. Hit Copy link above.
        2. In Chrome, click ⋮ (top right) → Settings.
        3. Click On startup → Open a specific page or set of pages → Add a new page → paste → Add.
        4. Then click Appearance → turn on Show home button → click the box under it and paste the link there too. Now the home button brings you back here.

          Notes

          Bring over your Apple Notes

          Apple keeps Notes inside its own apps, so there are two ways to move them here.

          1. Open a note in Apple Notes.
          2. Select all of it. On iPhone, tap and hold the text → Select All. On a Mac, press ⌘+A.
          3. Copy, then paste below. Lists, checklists and bold text come along.

          Moving lots of notes at once? On a Mac, free apps like Exporter from the Mac App Store save all your Apple Notes as files. Then pick them all here. Text, Markdown and HTML files work.
